# Trading Simulator

A plan for a simulator that teaches anyone 13 or older to invest and trade US stocks and options, including options strategies, using real tickers, synthetic prices and simulated money.

This repository holds the plan only: the product spec, the design guide, the lesson plan and the implementation plan. There is no app code yet. The only scripts are the ones in `docs/images/src/` that draw the diagrams; its README says how to run them.

## Documents

- **[Product spec](docs/spec.md):** what the app does, the level curriculum and unlock gates, architecture, costs, launch scope, the proposed refinements and the plan for markets beyond the US.
- **[UX and UI design guide](docs/design-guide.md):** design tokens, components, navigation, key screens, charts, microcopy, accessibility and PWA versus native differences.
- **[Lesson plan](docs/lesson-plan.md):** all four levels and the investing track, lesson by lesson, with timings, what each level unlocks, the learning loop and the farm-analogy glossary.
- **[Implementation plan](docs/implementation-plan.md):** phases, parallel workstreams with exclusive folder ownership, frozen interface contracts, merge rules, testing, risks, and the steps and costs that need Prafull.

## At a glance

- **Core loop:** a short lesson, then a replay modeled on a real market event, compressed into minutes, then a debrief of every replay from rule-based findings, worded by AI within a daily cap.
- **Prices:** real US stocks and ETFs by ticker and name, with every price generated by a market model and clearly labeled as simulated. No market data feed is bought.
- **Options:** unlocked through four gated levels, from stocks and ETFs up to spreads, priced with a labeled Black-Scholes model.
- **Investing:** a six-lesson track on goals, bonds, allocation and regular investing opens after Level 1.
- **Platforms:** one Flutter codebase for iOS, Android and an installable web app (PWA), with Firebase and Gemini server-side.
- **Launch:** free, with no payments, ads, chat or data selling.
- **Markets:** US and English at launch. Other markets are planned as market packs, and the contracts are market-ready before the build starts.

## Status

Spec v1, November 2026. A review on Nov 28, 2026 produced 31 refinements, listed in the [spec](docs/spec.md#review-refinements-nov-28-2026), and a plan for markets beyond the US (India, Mexico, Japan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Korea and others). Prafull decided all of them that day (decisions 22 to 42 in the spec). Everything accepted for version 1 is applied in every document; a strategy lab, investing-style lenses and four more investing lessons are accepted for after launch. The working product name, Ticker Camp, is not yet cleared by a trademark search, so this repository uses a generic name.

Nothing here is investment advice. The planned app uses simulated money only.
